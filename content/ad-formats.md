# Radical Edge Static Proof-Ad Formats

## Direction

There is no universal “best” static layout. Meta recommends creative diversification, 4:5 mobile framing, a single focal point, restrained image text, a clear value proposition, and an obvious action. Radical Edge should test several proof executions while keeping the evidence and interpretation consistent.

## 1. Result + proof stack — DEFAULT

The supplied reference is the default format.

- **Top 20%:** One large, specific result headline.
- **Middle 55%:** The strongest proof screenshot plus, only when approved, a relevant client/person cutout.
- **Bottom 25%:** What the proof means, a short masterclass invitation, and disclaimer.
- **Best for:** Revenue events, inbound wins, launch outcomes, strong WhatsApp proof.
- **Guardrail:** Replace “achieve this” with “see the system behind results like this”.

## 2. Message-first proof

- **Top:** Short recognition or inbound headline.
- **Middle:** One large WhatsApp/DM/email screenshot.
- **Bottom:** Interpretation and CTA.
- **Best for:** “Already convinced”, referral, or content-led inbound stories.

## 3. Testimonial pull-quote

- **Top:** Exact client quote, seven to fourteen words.
- **Middle:** Transcript excerpt or review card with attribution.
- **Bottom:** Mechanism, workshop CTA, result disclaimer.
- **Best for:** Qualitative transformation and authority positioning.

## 4. Analytics spotlight

- **Top:** One metric plus business relevance.
- **Middle:** Cropped analytics panel with a single highlighted metric.
- **Bottom:** Explain why the metric mattered commercially.
- **Best for:** Reach, saves, profile visits, qualified replies, or content-to-call attribution.

## 5. Before → shift → after

- **Top:** Outcome or transformation statement.
- **Middle:** Three compact stages with evidence at the “after” stage.
- **Bottom:** Name the system change, then CTA.
- **Best for:** Anonymised case studies with a clear starting point and timeframe.

## 6. Mini case-study card

- **Top:** Client category + observable result.
- **Middle:** Starting point, intervention, result, and proof snippet.
- **Bottom:** “How this happened” invitation.
- **Best for:** Complex results that need context to remain credible.

## Testing rule

For each proof story, create at least three genuinely different concepts—not just colour changes:

1. Evidence-first: screenshot dominates.
2. Result-first: headline dominates.
3. Interpretation-first: surprising business insight dominates.

Keep the offer, audience, proof, and attribution constant so the creative idea is the main variable.

## Research basis

- Meta recommends 1:1 or 4:5 framing for photo ads, simple layouts, a clear service value proposition, modern readable type, and concise copy.
- Meta’s photo-ad guidance recommends one focal point and restrained text.
- Meta’s Performance 5 guidance recommends creative diversification and systematic testing.
- Meta supports single-image and carousel formats; carousel is useful when proof needs a sequence or process.
